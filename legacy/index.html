About Dangau Sala D’Kontena

Dangau Sala D’Kontena is a small-scale resort located in Yan, Kedah, founded in 2016 by En. Hadi. Built on family-owned land, the resort combines modern container-style accommodation with traditional Malay elements to create a unique village experience.


The name “Dangau” reflects a simple village hut, symbolizing heritage, simplicity, and connection to nature. The resort focuses not only on accommodation, but also on creating meaningful experiences through community-based tourism and outdoor activities.
